# Meridian Capital v. Placeholder Logistics — Hearing Script (demo)

Fictional case, for demonstrating the Openings and Full script panes.

## OPENING 1 — Plaintiff's Motion to Compel

Your Honor, this motion concerns a narrow discovery dispute. Plaintiff served requests for
records that Defendant has treated as outside the scope the parties agreed to during the
meet-and-confer. We ask the Court to compel production of the outstanding categories.

## OPENING 2 — Defendant's Opposition

Your Honor, every document within the agreed scope has already been produced. Plaintiff is now
asking for records outside that scope, without ever having moved to expand it. The motion should
be denied, or at most limited to the categories the parties actually discussed.

## Background (reference only, not read aloud)

This section demonstrates content that `renderOpenings()` excludes (it only pulls the two
`## OPENING` sections) but `renderFullScript()` includes. A real hearing script would put
case-background analysis, exhibit summaries, or timeline notes here.
